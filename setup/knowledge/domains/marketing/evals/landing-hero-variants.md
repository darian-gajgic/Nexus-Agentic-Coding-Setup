Write the hero section (headline, subheadline, CTA button text, and one supporting proof
line) for the landing page of this product. FIXED evaluation brief: only the facts below;
missing facts become one-line stated assumptions; no invented numbers or testimonials.

FACTS (fixed):
- Product: a tool that turns messy client feedback from email/Slack into a prioritized
  feature list for small dev agencies.
- Audience: owners of 2–10-person dev agencies who lose feedback in threads and ship the
  wrong fixes first.
- Available proof: closed beta with 12 agencies; median time from "feedback arrives" to
  "ticket exists" dropped from 3 days to 20 minutes.
- Primary CTA: start the 14-day free trial (no card).

Follow the marketing playbook and house voice. Deliver: 3 headline variants (each with its
subheadline + CTA text + proof line), then mark which one you'd ship and WHY — the reason
must be tied to the audience, not taste.

Write a 3-email launch sequence (announce → value/objections → last call) for the following
offer. FIXED evaluation brief: only the facts below; missing facts become one-line stated
assumptions; never invent numbers or testimonials.

FACTS (fixed):
- Offer: "Ship Your First Client Site" — a 4-week live cohort teaching junior freelancers to
  scope, build and hand off a small-business website (max 20 seats, 349 €, starts November 4).
- Sender: a 2-person studio that has delivered 14 client sites in the last 18 months; students
  get the studio's real checklists (scoping call script, handoff doc, maintenance contract).
- List: 830 newsletter subscribers, mostly beginner freelancers; past launches converted ~2%.
- Objective: 20 seats sold by November 3, 23:59 CET.
- Proofs available: alumni result — Lena M. landed a 1.800 € site project 3 weeks after the last
  cohort; the maintenance-contract template alone has earned one alumnus 90 €/month recurring.
- Risk reversal: full refund until the end of week 1, no questions.
- Primary CTA (all three emails): enroll on the sales page.
- Constraints: subject lines ≤ 45 characters; single-column, skimmable; honest urgency only
  (the deadline and the 20-seat cap are real — nothing else is scarce).

Deliverable: three complete emails (subject, preview text, body, CTA), each with a one-line
note on its job in the sequence, plus a send-schedule line (day/time per email).

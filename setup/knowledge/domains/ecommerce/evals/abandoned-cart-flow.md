Design the 3-email abandoned-cart sequence for the shop below. FIXED evaluation brief: only
these facts; missing facts become stated assumptions; the discount policy is a hard constraint.

FACTS (fixed):
- Shop: small ceramics/woodwork studio shop (AOV 62 €, 68% of traffic mobile, checkout
  abandonment 74%); Klaviyo is available; sender identity: the two founders by first name.
- Products are handmade in small batches — batch scarcity is REAL (when a batch is gone,
  the listing pauses ~6 weeks) and may be used honestly.
- Discount policy (hard): no blanket discounts; a single 5-€-off code may appear ONLY in the
  final email and ONLY for carts ≥ 60 €.
- Support facts usable as objection-handlers: free DE shipping over 49 €; 30-day returns;
  every piece ships gift-wrapped by default; care instructions included.
- Objective: recover 8% of abandoned carts (currently 4% with a single generic reminder).
- Tracking: utm_campaign=cart-recovery, per-email utm_content.

Deliverable: the 3 emails (subject ≤ 45 chars, preview, body, one CTA each) + per email: send
timing with a one-line rationale, and the segment/exclusion rules (who must NOT get it —
purchasers, the discount-code email for carts < 60 €). End with the flow's success metric
definition and how to read it in 30 days.

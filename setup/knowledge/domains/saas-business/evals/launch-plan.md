Write the 4-week public-launch plan for the product below. FIXED evaluation brief: only these
facts; channel tactics must fit the ACTUAL audience assets listed — no imaginary communities.

FACTS (fixed):
- Product: Mahnwerk (payment reminders for German freelancers), 214 paying users from a quiet
  beta, pricing 12 €/month, self-serve, German-language product.
- Launch goal (proposed by founders, sanity-check it): 400 trials in launch month, ≥ 80 new
  paying customers by day 45.
- Audience assets (all real): newsletter 830 subscribers (~45% German freelancers); founder
  LinkedIn 2.1k followers (posts about freelancer finance get 3–5× median engagement);
  the pilot's 37 users incl. 6 who volunteered to be referenced; a guest-post relationship
  with one freelancer-finance blog (28k monthly readers); 0 € paid-ads budget this quarter.
- Team capacity for launch work: 10 h/week combined, 4 weeks.
- Constraints: no Product Hunt (English-first platform, German product); no discounting
  (price integrity rule); GDPR-clean referral mechanics only.

Deliverable: week-by-week plan (activities, owner-hours, expected contribution to the goal),
the launch narrative (one sentence: what's the story?), the referral/testimonial mechanics
using the 6 volunteers honestly, a goal sanity-check section (keep or revise the 400/80 with
arithmetic), and the 3 metrics reviewed weekly with kill/scale rules per channel.

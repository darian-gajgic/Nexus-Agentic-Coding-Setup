Write a pricing proposal for the SaaS below: keep, change, or restructure — with a defended
recommendation. FIXED evaluation brief: only these facts; every number in your proposal must
trace to one of them or be labeled as an assumption to validate.

FACTS (fixed):
- Product: Mahnwerk (automatic payment reminders for German freelancers). Current price:
  12 €/month flat, 14-day trial, no annual plan. 214 paying users, churn 4,1%/month,
  trial→paid 22%.
- Usage data: median user sends 9 reminders/month; p90 sends 34; 12 users (5,6%) are
  small agencies running reminders for multiple clients under one login, median 71/month.
- Support cost concentrates in the agency users (multi-client questions, CSV imports).
- Competitor anchors (public prices): sevDesk invoicing tier 9,90 €; Billomat 12 €;
  a dedicated dunning tool (Debitoor-successor) 24 € with per-client billing.
- Costs: infra ~0,40 €/user/month; reminder e-mails are near-zero marginal cost.
- Strategic goal next 12 months: grow MRR without increasing support load; the founders'
  hypothesis is the agency segment is underpriced but they fear churning them.

Deliverable: recommended structure (tiers, prices, packaging metric) with the reasoning
chain; what happens to the 12 agency accounts (grandfathering/migration plan with exact
comms timing); expected MRR impact as a range with the assumptions listed; the 3 risks and
their early-warning metrics; and what NOT to change and why.

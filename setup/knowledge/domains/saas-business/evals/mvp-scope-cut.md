Decide the MVP scope for the product below: what ships in v1, what explicitly waits, and why.
FIXED evaluation brief: only these facts; the wishlist is complete — do not add features.

FACTS (fixed):
- Product idea: "Handover" — the client-brain tool for 2–10 person agencies (per client: one
  pinned page for notes/decisions/credentials; search; export).
- Validated so far: 9 problem interviews with agency owners; 7/9 described a painful handover
  in the last year; 4/9 currently pay for Notion but say client info is "everywhere".
- Riskiest assumption (founders' own words): "agencies will maintain the client page weekly
  without being forced".
- Build capacity: one developer-equivalent, 10 weeks to a usable v1.
- The 14-item wishlist: client pages; global search; credential vault with encryption;
  meeting-notes email-in (forward a mail, it files itself); Slack capture; browser clipper;
  handover export (PDF/markdown); roles & permissions; client portal (clients see their page);
  billing/subscriptions; SSO; audit log; templates gallery; AI summary of each client page.
- Pricing intent: 29 €/month per workspace; first 10 design partners at 0 € for 3 months.

Deliverable: v1 scope (features in, each tied to the assumption it tests or the workflow it
completes), the explicit NOT-in-v1 list with the trigger that would pull each item in, the
walking-skeleton milestone plan for the 10 weeks (what works end-to-end at week 2/5/10), what
"validated" means at the end (metric + threshold + date), and the single most likely way this
MVP fails.

Write a 1.200–1.600 word article: "Stop chasing invoices by hand: a freelancer's system for
getting paid on time." FIXED evaluation brief: build the article ONLY on the facts below (they
are your verified research — cite them inline as given); anything beyond them must be a clearly
labeled opinion or a stated assumption, never a new statistic.

VERIFIED FACTS (treat the bracketed source as the citation to use):
1. 52% of B2B invoices in Germany are paid late [EOS "European Payment Practices" 2024].
2. The median German freelancer waits 32 days for payment on 14-day terms [Kontist study, 2023].
3. A structured 3-step reminder sequence (friendly nudge day 3, formal Mahnung day 14,
   final notice day 30) recovers 84% of late invoices without a collection agency
   [ifo/Creditreform panel, 2024].
4. Freelancers spend on average 3,5 hours/month on payment chasing [Kontist study, 2023].
5. German law does not require a Mahnung before default interest on B2B invoices —
   default occurs 30 days after the invoice by §286 BGB [BGB §286 (3)].
6. Late-payment interest for B2B is 9 percentage points above base rate [BGB §288 (2)].

Audience: German freelance creatives who invoice manually today. One core idea: getting paid
on time is a SYSTEM you set up once, not a monthly act of courage. End with one clear next
step (build the 3-step sequence this week — template included in the article).
Deliverable: the finished article with headline options (3), subheads that carry the skim
layer, the inline citations as given, and a 2-sentence meta description.

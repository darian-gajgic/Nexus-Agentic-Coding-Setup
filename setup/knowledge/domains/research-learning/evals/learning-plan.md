Design a 4-week learning plan: a junior marketer (runs our shop's organic social today,
never bought ads) learns to run Meta ads for a small D2C ceramics shop, at 5 hours/week,
with a 200 € total practice ad budget.

Rules of this evaluation brief:
- The plan must be practice-first: every week pairs learning material with a real artifact
  produced in the shop's ad account (campaign drafts count; spending starts week 2 at the
  earliest, capped 60 €/week).
- Every recommended resource must be a real, findable one (cite it: title + where), free
  unless unavoidable; no course-selling funnels.
- Weekly checkpoints must be falsifiable ("can explain X" is not checkable — "has built X,
  reviewed against Y checklist" is).
- Include the failure branch: what to do at each checkpoint if it's not met.
- End with: what the junior should be able to do unaided after week 4 (3 concrete abilities),
  the 3 most common beginner mistakes in Meta ads for small shops (sourced), and the metric
  that tells us in week 6 whether the 200 € taught us anything.

Deliverable: the 4-week plan, checkpoint table, and the closing sections above.

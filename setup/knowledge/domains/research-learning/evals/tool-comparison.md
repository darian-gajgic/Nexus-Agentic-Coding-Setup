Research and write a comparison report: the current tool landscape for automated payment
reminders (Mahnwesen) available to German freelancers. Compare at least 4 real tools on:
price, reminder-sequence automation depth, legal compliance features (Mahnung stages,
Verzugszinsen), invoicing-suite lock-in vs standalone, and fit for a 1-person business.

Rules of this evaluation brief:
- Live research is expected — every load-bearing claim carries an inline source + access date,
  and exactly one confidence label from [confirmed] / [likely] / [uncertain].
- Prices must come from the vendor's public pricing page (cite it), dated.
- Where sources conflict, show both and say which you weight and why.
- End with: a one-table summary; a recommendation for the persona "freelance designer,
  15 invoices/month, wants zero manual dunning"; the 3 questions a buyer must verify
  themselves in a trial (things public sources cannot settle).

Deliverable: the report, max ~1.200 words + the table, in the house report voice.

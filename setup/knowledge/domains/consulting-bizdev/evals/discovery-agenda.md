Prepare the discovery-call plan for the inbound lead below: agenda, question script, and
qualification scorecard. FIXED evaluation brief: only these facts; the plan must be usable
by a junior running the call alone.

FACTS (fixed):
- Lead came through the website form: "Hi, we're relaunching our sports-nutrition D2C brand
  (currently on WooCommerce, moving to Shopify?) and need someone for the shop migration +
  maybe branding + maybe email flows. Timeline: yesterday :) — Deniz, COO, PowerFuel GmbH."
- Public facts: PowerFuel sells protein products, ~90 SKUs incl. flavor/size variants and
  subscriptions; Instagram 28k followers; a "Klarna financing" badge on the current shop;
  imprint shows 11 employees, Hamburg.
- Our offer landscape: we sell fixed-scope sprints (audit 2.400 €, migration sprints from
  8.000 €); we do NOT take open-ended agency-of-record work; subscriptions on Shopify need a
  third-party app — we've done exactly one such migration (60 SKUs, no subscriptions).
- Red flags to probe: "yesterday" timeline, three unscoped workstreams, subscription migration
  complexity, decision authority (COO vs founder?).

Deliverable: a 30-minute agenda with time boxes; 12 questions in priority order, each with the
one line WHY it's asked and what answer disqualifies; a scorecard (5 criteria, pass/fail
definitions); the walk-away rule stated plainly; and the two honest next-step options we can
offer given our actual experience level.

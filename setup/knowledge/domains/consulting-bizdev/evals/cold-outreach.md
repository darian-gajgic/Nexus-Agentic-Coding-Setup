Write ONE cold outreach email (plus a 2-sentence LinkedIn connection note variant) to the
prospect below. FIXED evaluation brief: only these facts; nothing invented about the prospect;
missing facts become stated assumptions inside the deliverable's notes, not inside the email.

FACTS (fixed):
- We are a 2-person studio selling a productized offer: "Shop-Tune" — a 14-day conversion
  audit + fix sprint for small Shopify stores, 2.400 € fixed, deliverables: audit report,
  10 highest-leverage fixes implemented, before/after metrics doc. Two prior clients:
  a tea shop (checkout completion +19% in 6 weeks) and a stationery brand (mobile PDP
  bounce −24%). We can only take 2 sprints/month.
- Prospect: Mara Lindqvist, founder of "Fjell & Fern" (outdoor-lifestyle prints and apparel,
  Shopify, ~15k monthly sessions per SimilarWeb tier estimate). Signals we actually observed:
  (a) her checkout requires account creation, (b) product pages load a 4 MB hero video on
  mobile, (c) she posted on LinkedIn 12 days ago that Q3 revenue was flat despite growing
  traffic and asked "where do people drop off?".
- Ask: a 20-minute call next week to walk her through the 3 drop-off suspects we already see.

Deliverable: subject line, the email (≤ 150 words, references at least two observed signals,
exactly one ask, no attachment, honest about who we are and capacity), the LinkedIn variant,
and one line on the follow-up plan if she doesn't reply (when + angle).

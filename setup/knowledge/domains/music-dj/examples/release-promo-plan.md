## Why this works

- **4-week runway is set by the slowest gate, not by patience** (PLAYBOOK task 6): distributor delivery + editorial pitch eligibility need lead time; uploading at D-25 and pitching at D-21 turns platform rules into non-issues (junior mistake 12).
- **Every action has a date and an owner** — the RUBRIC release gate. "TBD" is on the kill list because unowned actions silently don't happen in a 2-person team running four business lines.
- **The presave link exists before the announce post** (rubric gate): announcing with nothing to click burns the single biggest attention spike of the cycle.
- **One shoot (D-23) feeds the whole calendar**: batching footage is what makes a 2-person promo cycle sustainable next to SaaS/client work (BUSINESS-CONTEXT.md workload reality).
- **The D-7 checkpoint is numeric with a pre-decided response** (RUBRIC dimension 7 at level 4): "below 100 presaves → 2 extra posts + 10 DJ DMs, no ad spend" removes panic decisions mid-cycle.
- **DJ promo runs 1 week before public announce**: DJs playing it pre-release generates the crowd clips that become D+4 content — the promo feeds itself (PLAYBOOK principle 10, catalog compounding).
- **Pitch email is under 150 words with the link above the fold**: curators triage in seconds; the personalized first line is the only part that beats the delete key. One follow-up cap protects the sender reputation for the NEXT release.
- **Retro at D+14 into the feedback folder is a hard action**: the plan is a loop, not a line — three carried lessons per cycle is how juniors compound to professional level.

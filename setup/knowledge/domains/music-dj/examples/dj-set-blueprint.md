# 90-Minute Peak-Time Set Blueprint

**Scenario:** Saturday club night, 350-cap room, peak-time slot 01:00–02:30, house/techno floor.
Warm-up DJ hands over at ~124 BPM, floor already moving. A closer follows you — you do NOT have to end the night, you have to hand over a hot but not exhausted floor.
Built per PLAYBOOK.md → Task playbook 4; gates per RUBRIC.md (set plan section).

## Energy arc (1–10 scale, rated per PLAYBOOK P-scale)

```

 7 |       ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ##
 6 | ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ## ##
   +---------------------------------------------------------
     0        15       30       45       60       75       90  (min)
     |lock-in |wave 1     |R1|wave 2      |R2|final wave   |land
```

Three waves, two planned resets (R1 ~min 36, R2 ~min 58). The maximum (10) lands at min 75–80, NOT at the end — the last 10 minutes ride down so the closer DJ has somewhere to go.

## Lane plan

- **BPM lane:** enter 126 → drift up to 131 at peak 3 → land 128. No silent jump > 2 BPM; every bigger move happens inside a breakdown or cut.
- **Key lane (Camelot):** one clockwise drift 8A → 12A across the first hour, a designed jump to 5A at the vocal moment (full FX cut — key rules don't apply across silence), then 5A → 10A drift to the close. Letter swaps (A→B) reserved for the brightest moments.
- **Crate size:** 60 tracks loaded for 20 slots (3× rule), organized in role playlists: OPENERS(6) / GROOVERS(14) / LIFTERS(10) / PEAK BOMBS(8) / RESETS(6) / CLOSERS(4) / SAFE(6) / WILDCARDS(6).

## Pre-set check (10 min before, per PLAYBOOK task 5)

1. USB loaded and browsing on the actual player; backup USB confirmed in pocket.
2. Gains flat, EQs at noon, cue level low; recorder running.
3. Outgoing DJ's current BPM/key noted; first track cued at pad 1.

## Booth execution notes (per phase)

**Lock-in (slots 1–3, min 0–13)**
- Match the outgoing DJ's level exactly (meter, not ears — the room deafens you for the first 5 min).
- No FX, no loops, no key tricks. The job is trust: prove the groove continues.
- Watch: is the floor holding through track 2? Holding = proceed; thinning = Branch A immediately.

**Wave 1 (slots 4–8, min 13–36)**
- Introduce one technique per transition, never two (echo out OR loop stack OR bass swap).
- Gain check at every mix-in: channel meters peak amber; the club limiter is not your friend.
- PEAK 1 rule: commit. Fader fully open, hands off for 90 seconds, watch the room, not the gear.

**Reset 1 (slot 9, min 36–40)**
- Announce the valley with a long filter-down — a reset that sounds like a mistake reads as a trainwreck.
- Expect and accept the bar run. Count who stays locked in: those are your people for wave 2.

**Wave 2 + vocal moment (slots 10–13, min 40–58)**
- The FX cut into slot 12 is rehearsed at home: echo tail length set, both channels' EQ pre-positioned.
- If the acapella moment lands (singing), let slot 12 run 16 bars longer than planned before PEAK 2.

**Reset 2 + final wave (slots 14–18, min 58–81)**
- This reset is longer and deeper; resist any request pressure here (see rule below).
- Slots 15–16 rebuild patience: two full grooves before liftoff, even if the floor begs.
- PEAK 3: this is the photo moment. Pre-check: phone lights = yes; if the room peaked early, swap in the banked SAFE bomb instead of forcing All Caps.

**Land (slots 19–20, min 81–90)**
- Start easing gain down 1–2 dB across the last 2 tracks — the closer opens with headroom.
- Handover protocol: tell the closer last-track BPM/key at least 5 min out; end on a rolling groove, never on silence.

## Contingency branches

**BRANCH A — crowd colder than expected**
Trigger (checkable): floor < 60% full at min 20, OR no visible hand response at PEAK 1.
1. Do NOT push harder — that empties floors. Drop back to GROOVERS lane at energy 7.
2. Delay peak 1 to ~min 35; replace slots 6–7 with two SAFE-list groovers.
3. Cut RESET 1 entirely (a cold floor needs no cooling), keep RESET 2 shortened.
4. Re-enter the printed plan at slot 10; accept a lower ceiling: peak 3 at energy 9 is a win on a cold night.

**BRANCH B — crowd hotter than expected**
Trigger: sustained hands-up + singing along before min 20.
1. Bring PEAK 2 material forward one slot — but ADD a reset, never delete one (burnout by 02:00 = dead handover).
2. Cap the lane at 131 BPM anyway; speed is not energy.
3. Bank one PEAK BOMB unplayed for a possible one-more-tune; tell the closer if you didn't use it.

**BRANCH C — technical failure**
1. Player/USB dies: second USB is in the left jacket pocket — swap into the working player, loop current track meanwhile (pad 3 = breakdown loop on every track, per cue standard).
2. Both players suspect: phone + emergency 60-min mix via booth aux/Bluetooth while tech resets.
3. After any failure: next 2 tracks from SAFE list, zero experiments, rebuild trust.

**BRANCH D — booth monitor dead or unusable**
1. Do not ride the headphone cue louder to compensate — that's how ears get damaged and mixes drift.
2. Trust the beatgrid: sync-check visually, mix shorter (8–16 bar transitions), favor cut transitions over long blends.
3. Tell the promoter at the first gap; log it in the gig notes — a recurring monitor problem changes the fee conversation next time.

**Requests / promoter pressure rule:** one polite deferral ("got it lined up later"), play it only if it fits the current lane ±2 BPM and role; never during a reset.

## Why this works

- **The max is at min 75–80, not 90** (PLAYBOOK principle 7 + slot rules): peak-time slot ends with a handover, so the arc lands at 8 — the closer inherits momentum instead of ashes.
- **Two planned resets** make three peaks possible. Without R1/R2 this is a monotonic climb — a RUBRIC kill-list item — and PEAK 3 would read as "more of the same" instead of the biggest moment.
- **First 3 + last 2 tracks are fixed, the middle is lanes** (Playbook 4.7): slots 4–17 name tracks for rehearsal value, but in the booth each is "role + BPM + key window", so crowd-reading (Playbook 4.8–4.9) can override without breaking the arc.
- **Every BPM move > 1 happens inside a breakdown, cut, or FX moment** — the floor never hears the tempo change, satisfying the "no silent jump > 3" rubric gate with margin.
- **The 12A → 5A jump is legal because of silence**: harmonic rules constrain overlapping audio; a full FX cut resets the key context. This is the designed "phones-up" moment, placed at min 49 — deep enough that the room is bought in.
- **The +2 Camelot boost (7A → 9A) is executed percussively** — the one place theory says a lift works is exactly where melodic content is thinnest.
- **Contingency triggers are observable** (floor %, hands, singing) not vibes — an agent or a nervous junior can check them mid-set, per RUBRIC dimension 8.
- **SAFE list is 6 tracks with cues set** — recovery from any flop or failure is one button press away, which is what makes experimenting in the middle safe at all.

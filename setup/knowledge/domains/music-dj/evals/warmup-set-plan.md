SITUATION (fixed):
- Venue: 150-cap basement club, Leipzig, single warm room, decent but bass-heavy system.
- Slot: 23:00–00:30, doors 23:00 — the room starts EMPTY and must be at a simmering,
  not boiling, energy when the headliner (melodic house, plays ~121–123 BPM) takes over.
- Alias: VELT (melodic/organic house). House rule: full harmonic mixing; no peak-time
  anthems in the warm-up; the headliner's known recent set openers sit around 121 BPM in 8A/9A.
- Crowd: local heads + the headliner's following; phones come out for deep moments, not drops.

Deliverable: (1) the arc described in 3 phases with target BPM/energy per phase and the
handover state at 00:30; (2) a 20–24 track pool organized by phase (real tracks; label + year;
intended key neighborhood per the Camelot wheel, marked "verify"), (3) transition notes for
the 5 riskiest moments (why risky, technique), (4) the reading-the-room plan: 3 observable
signals and the pre-decided reaction to each, (5) recording + post-set breakdown checklist
(the VELT content ritual).

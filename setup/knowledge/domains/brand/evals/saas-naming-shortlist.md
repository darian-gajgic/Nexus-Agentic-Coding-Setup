Produce a naming shortlist and a defended recommendation for the product below. FIXED
evaluation brief: only these facts; where a real-world check (trademark, domain) cannot be
performed, state exactly what must be verified and how — do not claim it was done.

FACTS (fixed):
- Product: a lightweight internal-knowledge tool for 2–10 person agencies: every client gets
  one page ("the client brain") that meeting notes, decisions and credentials pin to; search
  across clients; hand-off mode that exports a client brain to a successor.
- Category language customers use: "wiki", "notion for client work", "handover doc".
- Positioning core: the tool that makes any teammate replaceable in a day — knowledge lives
  with the client, not with the person.
- Tone of the brand: dry, competent, slightly wry; German founders, international audience.
- Constraints: must work spoken aloud in German AND English; .com or .app obtainable
  (assume common dictionary-word .coms are gone); no "-ly", no "get-" prefix; avoid genericisms
  (hub, base, mind) unless twisted.

Deliverable: 12 candidates in 3 naming routes (descriptive / metaphor / coined), each with a
one-line rationale and a rejection reason where it fails screening; a 3-name shortlist scored
against 6 explicit criteria in a table; one recommended name with a 5-sentence defense and the
exact verification checklist (trademark class, domain, social handles, German pronunciation).

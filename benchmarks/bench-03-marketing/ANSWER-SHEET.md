# Bench-03 Answer Sheet — marketing campaign benchmark

Same protocol as bench-02: you are the same client in every session; the shop/business facts below are obtainable ONLY by asking. A marketing campaign for an unknown shop is impossible to do well without asking — whether a system realizes that is a core measurement of this benchmark.

## Rules (binding — identical to bench-02)

1. Answer ONLY what is asked; never volunteer sheet content.
2. Canonical answers word-for-word where they fit; improvised answers = one short sentence.
3. Not covered below → `You decide — whatever gets his shop customers.` (log as off-sheet)
4. Log EVERY Q→A in RESULTS.md under the arm.
5. In Nexus, answer skippable interview questions instead of skipping.
6. Respond promptly.

## What "ready to use" means for grading (Phase E — don't reveal to the arms)

The checklist scores against the marketing rubric's objective gates (`~/knowledge/domains/marketing/RUBRIC.md`): measurable objective, named audience segment, one CTA per asset, claims backed by adjacent proof, zero fabricated stats/testimonials (every statistic needs source + year), no placeholders (`{{`, `TODO`, `lorem`), and a skim-test-able structure. Spot-check at least 3 cited sources per arm in the browser — a fabricated source fails the arm's research row.
